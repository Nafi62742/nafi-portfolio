## ⚡ Quick-Start: 3 Steps to Launch Today

### Step 1: Polish Your Profile Foundation (30 mins)
1. Open [`01_PROFILE_UPGRADE_PLAYBOOK.md`](./01_PROFILE_UPGRADE_PLAYBOOK.md).
2. Choose one of the 3 **Headlines** and paste it into your profile.
3. Replace your current About section with **Bio Option 1 (Story + Impact)** or **Bio Option 2 (Technical Deep Dive)**.
4. Pin your top 5 technical skills for recruiter algorithmic search: `Flutter`, `Angular`, `AWS Lambda`, `TypeScript`, `Dart`.

### Step 2: Post Your First Project Showcase (15 mins)
1. Open [`03_READY_TO_POST_PROJECTS.md`](./03_READY_TO_POST_PROJECTS.md).
2. Pick **Post 1: RunMate Club** (Marathon GPS engine) or **Post 2: Koyama Shokai** (Angular 21 + AWS Serverless).
3. Attach a 20–45 second screen recording or clean architecture diagram (see [`05_MEDIA_AND_CAROUSEL_CREATION.md`](./05_MEDIA_AND_CAROUSEL_CREATION.md)).
4. Hit Publish during the peak engagement window (9:00 AM – 11:30 AM BST).

### Step 3: Fuel the Algorithm (15 mins after posting)
1. Reply immediately to the first 3 comments to signal high engagement velocity to the feed algorithm.
2. Follow the networking routine in [`04_NETWORKING_ALGORITHM_STRATEGY.md`](./04_NETWORKING_ALGORITHM_STRATEGY.md).
