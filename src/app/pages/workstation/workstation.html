Why the 3x Front Intake + DeepCool AG400 Tower Push + 1x Rear Exhaust configuration was engineered specifically for high thermal stability and dust immunity in the darkFlash Leo chassis.

The Physics of Positive Air Pressure

Total Intake CFM > Total Exhaust CFM

In a positive pressure environment, more air is forced into the chassis than is extracted by exhaust fans. The excess air volume builds a slight internal positive gradient, forcing air outwards through every microscopic seam, PCIe bracket slot, and vent.

Key Advantage: Because 100% of incoming air passes through the darkFlash Leo front mesh dust filter, zero dust enters through unfiltered chassis gaps.

Linear Wind-Tunnel Flow Path

Front Hex Mesh → DeepCool AG400 → Rear Exhaust

Unlike cases with choked front glass or turbulent multi-directional fans, the darkFlash Leo with DeepCool AG400 creates an uninterrupted horizontal wind tunnel. The AG400 fan is mechanically in-line with the top front intake and rear exhaust fan.

Thermal Headroom: Allows the Intel i5-10400F to sustain maximum boost frequencies indefinitely with core temperatures staying safely below 65°C under synthetic loads.

RTX 3060 Direct Fresh Air Delivery

Front Fan #2 Dedicated Intake Stream

Front intake fan #2 is positioned precisely level with the graphics card's triple axial fans. The fans draw fresh air from below and drive airflow directly upward through the heatsink and vented backplate into the top/rear exhaust stream, preventing heat recirculation.

0dB Fan Stop: During programming, writing code, or web browsing, the GPU fans can completely turn off (0 RPM) while ambient case airflow keeps the GPU core idling under 40°C.

Independent Thermal Shroud (PSU)

Underside Intake → Direct Rear Exhaust

The 750W power supply is housed inside a dedicated bottom metal tunnel. It draws cold air from underneath the case through an easily cleanable dust filter and exhausts heat directly out the back, completely isolated from the CPU and GPU chamber.

Zero Thermal Spill: Power supply transformation heat never enters or warms up the CPU or GPU cooling cycles.
